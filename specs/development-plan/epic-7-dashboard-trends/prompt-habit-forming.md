# Prompt for advice on encouraging habit forming

## Gemini

I'm making a gym workout tracking app. I'm interested in insights from reputable research on forming and maintaining beneficial habits, specifically how best to present the user's historical habit data to them, in order to boost motivation. The key for me is to focus on building up a cadence and regular frequency of workouts, rather than focussing on lifting heavier. I believe the former will naturally lead to the latter, with fewer injuries. I want to present the user's recent frequency of workouts, say over the last 28 days, and possibly with comparison to a longer time period. But I want it pitched to not put pressure or apply negative motivation eg guilt at missing workouts. I want to highlight any patterns of regular frequency.
Provide some suggestions on how a digital app could present this kind of data.
